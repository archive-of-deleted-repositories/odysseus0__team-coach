# Generate Weekly Newsletter

You are tasked with generating a comprehensive weekly newsletter from the provided meeting transcripts and chat logs. 

## Instructions

1. **Process all provided files** to extract key information
2. **Organize content** into the following sections:
   - 📍 The Week's Highlights (3-5 most important items)
   - 🎯 Progress & Momentum (project updates and achievements)
   - 💡 Interesting Discussions (technical insights and debates)
   - 🚧 Heads Up (blockers, needs, upcoming decisions)
   - 🎪 The Human Side (team moments, funny interactions)
   - 🔗 Resources to Check Out (links shared)

3. **Writing Style**:
   - Be concise but informative
   - Use bullet points for easy scanning
   - Include specific names and details
   - Maintain a professional yet friendly tone

## Output Format

Generate a markdown document with:
- Clear section headers with emojis
- Bullet points for each item
- Specific details (names, decisions, dates)
- No generic statements - be specific

## Focus Areas

- Key decisions and their rationale
- Project progress and blockers
- Technical discussions and insights
- Team dynamics and collaboration
- Action items and responsibilities

Start your output with:
# Weekly Team Newsletter
